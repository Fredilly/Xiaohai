# #129 Illustrated visual review / 插画视觉评审

The 15 SVG assets in this directory are **design review frames**, not real browser or WeChat screenshots. They show the chosen children's storybook hero plus flat icon tiles, without introducing new app features. Each has Home/Global/Me in narrow (375 × 812) and wide (430 × 932) design sizes, plus narrow loading/empty/error variants.
